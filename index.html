WHAT WE'RE ACTUALLY LOOKING AT

Customer experience isn't the website. It's everything that happens before and after someone buys.

For Orchard Piper, that's the enquiry, the selling agent's response, the display suite, years of construction updates, the handover, and life inside the building years later. Customers don't experience these as separate departments. They experience one relationship.

HOW WE MEASURE IT

Every finding gets a label, so it's clear what's actually at stake.

Not just "this feels off." Each issue is tagged as one of three things, based on what we actually test, real enquiries, timed responses, and comparisons against what's promised.

Trust

Does the experience match what's promised. A gap between the brand's claim and what a customer actually gets.

Conversion

Where a real decision gets harder than it needs to be. A slow reply, an unclear next step, a moment of doubt.

Loyalty

What happens after the sale. Whether the relationship holds up, or fades once the transaction is done.

A WORKED EXAMPLE

Here's the method, on your own journey.

A preview, not the findings themselves, those come from the audit. This is how every stage gets read once the work begins.

TRUST
Enquiry
Does the response match the exacting-standards promise on the website, or read like any other agency reply.
CONVERSION
Sales experience
Sales run through a third-party team, not Orchard Piper directly. Conversion happens here, in the display suite and the follow-up calls. How well they close reflects on Orchard Piper's brand either way.
TRUST
Construction
A builder engaged by Orchard Piper carries that reputation for two and a half years or more. Professionalism has to hold through the entire build, not just the sales pitch.
LOYALTY
Post-settlement
Loyalty is measured twice, once in whether the promise of hotel style living survives daily life, and again in whether the builder remains accountable long after handover.
Sales across all four projects run through external agency teams, not Orchard Piper directly, which is exactly why conversion is the stage worth watching most closely.
