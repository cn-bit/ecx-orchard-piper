HOW WE MEASURE IT

Every finding is tagged, so it's clear what's at stake.

Each issue is classed as one of three things, based on what we test directly, real enquiries, timed responses, and comparisons against what's promised.

Trust

Does the experience match what's promised. A gap between the brand's claim and what a customer actually gets.

Conversion

Where a real decision gets harder than it needs to be. A slow reply, an unclear next step, a moment of doubt.

Loyalty

Whether the relationship strengthens after purchase, through construction, handover and life in the building, or fades once the contract is signed.

THE PROCESS

Four weeks, and what we'll need from you.

Where Orchard Piper's time and access are needed, stage by stage.

WEEK 1
Kickoff
Founder, Head of Sales and Marketing, Head of Design and Development Manager. Sets priorities, confirms access, and agrees the measures that define success.
WEEKS 1 TO 3
Fieldwork
Enquiries tested and touchpoints reviewed across all four projects. Needs access to construction and delivery partners and past purchasers. Selling agents join a debrief once enquiry testing is complete.
WEEK 3
Synthesis
Findings tagged and mapped against commercial priority. No Orchard Piper time required.
WEEKS 3 TO 4
Delivery
Walkthrough with leadership, and the review date agreed. Whoever owns follow-through needs to be in the room.
A follow-up review, measured against the agreed scorecard, closes the loop.
